## 📌 Project Overview 
A new fitness startup wants to launch an app for busy office professionals who struggle to find time for exercise.
The goal of this project was to step into the role of a UX Researcher and develop a clear understanding of the target user before designing the product.

The project required the development of a user persona and an empathy map to explore the user’s goals, frustrations, thoughts, feelings, and behaviours.

**🎯 Target Audience**

Busy, time-poor office professionals who want to maintain an active and healthy lifestyle but struggle to find time for exercise because of demanding work schedules.

## 🧩 What's Inside
- **👤 User Persona**
- Alex Wisdom, a 27-year-old Project Manager, including his *🎯 Goals* and *😤 Frustrations*
  
- **🧠 Empathy Map — A 4-quadrant map**

The empathy map explores the user’s overall experience through four key quadrants: 

* 💬 Says
* 👀 Does
* 🧠 Thinks and 
* ❤️ Feels,

providing a holistic understanding of what the user communicates, how they behave, what they believe or consider, and the emotions they experience throughout their journey.

## 🔑 Key Pain

“I want to stay active, but my work schedule makes it difficult to find and maintain time for exercise.”
The central challenge is not a lack of interest in exercise. The user’s demanding work schedule makes it difficult to maintain a consistent and sustainable exercise routine.
## 💡 Key UX Insight

The user’s challenge is not a lack of interest in exercise, but the difficulty of finding a realistic and sustainable way to stay active within a demanding work schedule.
This insight provides a potential direction for future design decisions: the fitness experience should fit naturally into the user’s existing routine rather than feel like another major commitment.

## 🎨 Design Consideration

The findings from this project can help guide future product decisions by focusing on solutions that are:

* Time-efficient
* Easy to incorporate into a busy schedule
* Realistic and sustainable
* Simple to understand and use
* Supportive rather than overwhelming

## 🧠 UX Process

1. Reviewed the project scenario and identified the target audience: busy, time-poor office professionals.
2. Defined a specific user persona representing the target audience.
3. Identified the persona’s goals and frustrations.
4. Mapped the user’s Says, Does, Thinks, and Feels.
5. Synthesized the findings into a Key Pain and Key UX Insight.
6. Used the findings to establish a clearer understanding of the user before moving into the design stage.
